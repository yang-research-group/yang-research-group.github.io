Principal Investigator

Prof. Yan-Jie Yang

Principal Investigator (PI) · Professor of Accounting Discipline
College of Management, Yuan Ze University

The Yang Research Group conducts empirical and computational research in accounting, auditing, capital markets, corporate governance, and ESG. Our research integrates large-scale financial and corporate datasets with econometric methods, computational analysis, and artificial intelligence to examine how information, regulation, governance, and emerging technologies shape corporate behavior and capital markets.

Main Research Areas

Empirical Auditing Research

Investigating auditor behavior, audit quality mechanisms, pricing strategies, and the institutional impacts of regulatory oversight across public accounting firms.

Financial Accounting and Capital Markets

Examining the informational role of corporate financial reports, earnings quality dynamics, and market pricing efficiency across complex business networks.

Corporate Governance and ESG

Analyzing how board oversight mechanisms, ownership architectures, and ESG disclosures influence agency costs, corporate sustainability, and long-term enterprise value.

Artificial Intelligence and Accounting Research

Examining how artificial intelligence affects accounting information, corporate decision-making, professional practice, and capital markets.

Computational and Econometric Methods

Applying econometric, computational, and large-scale data methods to empirical accounting and capital-market research.

Professor

Prof. Yanjie Yang

Faculty of Management, Yuan Ze University

Our group focuses on rigorous empirical research in accounting, business strategy, and financial markets. We aim to understand the complex dynamics of financial information and its impact on corporate behavior.

YZU Official Profile ORCID: 0000-0001-7346-5050

Main Research Areas

Financial Accounting

Investigating the quality of financial reporting and its implications for capital markets.

Corporate Governance

Analyzing the role of board structures, executive compensation, and institutional investors.

Auditing

Examining auditor behavior, audit quality, and regulatory impacts on the auditing profession.

Ongoing Projects

Active

Board Diversity and Corporate Governance Evaluation

An empirical analysis of board structures and their impact on corporate governance metrics among major firms.

Active

Market Collusion and Stock Price Dynamics

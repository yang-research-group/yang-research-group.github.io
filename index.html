Advancing Empirical and Computational Research in Accounting & Capital Markets

The Yang Research Group conducts empirical and computational research in accounting, auditing, capital markets, corporate governance, and ESG. Our research integrates large-scale financial and corporate datasets with econometric methods, computational analysis, and artificial intelligence to examine how information, regulation, governance, and emerging technologies shape corporate behavior and capital markets.

Main Research Areas

Empirical Auditing Research

Examining audit quality, auditor behavior, regulatory oversight, and the informational role of assurance in capital markets.

Financial Accounting and Capital Markets

Studying financial reporting quality, disclosure, earnings information, and their effects on market pricing and investor response.

Corporate Governance and ESG

Analyzing board structures, ownership, executive incentives, sustainability reporting, and broader ESG-related corporate outcomes.

Artificial Intelligence and Accounting Research

Exploring how machine learning, natural language processing, and AI applications can transform accounting analysis and decision-making.

Computational and Econometric Methods

Developing and applying advanced computational, statistical, and econometric techniques to analyze large-scale financial and corporate datasets.

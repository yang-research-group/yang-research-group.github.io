Ongoing Research Works

Advancing empirical and computational research across accounting, corporate governance, capital markets, and data intelligence.

Empirical Auditing Financial Accounting & Capital Markets Corporate Governance & ESG AI & Accounting Research Computational & Econometric Methods

Our research interests are focused on advancing empirical and computational accounting, corporate governance, capital markets, and data intelligence through econometric modeling and artificial intelligence methodologies. Specifically, our work can be categorized into the following themes:

Theme 01

Empirical Auditing Research

Investigating auditor behavior, audit quality mechanisms, pricing strategies, and the institutional impacts of regulatory oversight across public accounting firms.

Key Research Directions:

  • Auditor Choice & Audit Fee Premium: Examining how corporate ownership structures (including family-controlled enterprises and state-owned firms) and split-share structural reforms influence auditor selection, risk assessment, and audit fee premiums.
  • Audit Partner Dynamics & Job-Hopping: Tracing the micro-level dynamics of audit partner mobility, continuing partner–client relationships, and regulatory enforcement on audit quality and market credibility.
  • Risk-Based Auditing Frameworks: Evaluating how industry specialization, client economic importance, and non-audit service provisions affect audit risk evaluation, restatement likelihood, and audit failure mitigation.
Associated Grants:
NSTC / MOST Audit Pricing in Chinese Family Firms Audit Partner Mobility Dynamics
Theme 02

Financial Accounting and Capital Markets

Examining the informational role of corporate financial reports, earnings quality dynamics, and market pricing efficiency across complex business networks.

Key Research Directions:

  • Earnings Quality & Discretionary Accruals: Modeling corporate disclosure transparency, earnings management behaviors, and accounting signal fidelity in shaping investor expectations and cost of capital.
  • Market Pricing Efficiency & Asset Valuation: Analyzing how financial statement signals, financial analyst forecast revisions, and macroeconomic disclosures drive asset valuation and risk discovery.
  • Supply Chain Information Spillover: Investigating disclosure and shock transmission across customer-supplier trade networks and their implications for credit ratings and corporate financial health.
Associated Grants:
Capital Market Earnings Signals & Pricing Models Supply Chain Financial Contagion
Theme 03

Corporate Governance and ESG

Analyzing how board oversight mechanisms, ownership architectures, and ESG disclosures influence agency costs, corporate sustainability, and long-term enterprise value.

Key Research Directions:

  • Board Independence & Fiduciary Oversight: Assessing independent director effectiveness, audit committee oversight, executive compensation incentives, and internal controls in mitigating managerial entrenchment.
  • ESG Disclosures & Sustainability Assurance: Evaluating how non-financial ESG disclosures, carbon emission reporting, and sustainability ratings impact equity valuation and institutional capital allocation.
  • Controlling Shareholder Dynamics & Investor Voice: Investigating controlling shareholder expropriation, institutional investor monitoring, shareholder activism, and corporate risk-taking behavior.
Associated Grants:
Corporate Governance & Executive Compensation Structure ESG Ratings & Cost of Equity Capital
Theme 04

Artificial Intelligence and Accounting Research

“Examining how artificial intelligence affects accounting information, corporate decision-making, professional practice, and capital markets.”

Key Research Directions:

  • AI in Corporate Decision-Making & Capital Markets: Investigating the integration of artificial intelligence systems in financial reporting pipelines, automated algorithmic trading reactions, and strategic management decisions.
  • Financial NLP & Unstructured Disclosure Analysis: Applying natural language processing, transformer architectures, and large language models (LLMs) to corporate 10-K/10-Q filings, MD&A narratives, and earnings call transcripts.
  • Machine Learning for Predictive Accounting & Audit Risk: Developing predictive models for accounting fraud detection, financial distress early warning, restatement prediction, and automated audit risk classification.
Associated Grants:
AI & Corporate Financial Disclosures Project Machine Learning in Audit Quality & Risk Forecasting
Theme 05

Computational and Econometric Methods

“Applying econometric, computational, and large-scale data methods to empirical accounting and capital-market research.”

Key Research Directions:

  • Empirical Capital-Market Modeling & Big Data: Processing large-scale corporate records, trading transactions, and longitudinal databases (TEJ, CSMAR, Compustat) with high-performance computational workflows.
  • Causal Inference & Identification Strategies: Implementing difference-in-differences (DiD), instrumental variables (IV), panel fixed-effects, and regression discontinuity designs (RDD) to address endogeneity and establish causality.
  • Microstructure & High-Frequency Analytics: Examining intraday order flow dynamics, market liquidity shocks, and high-frequency informational efficiency across major Asian and global exchanges.
Associated Grants:
Econometric Frameworks for Capital Market Microstructure Advanced Panel Identification in Empirical Accounting
